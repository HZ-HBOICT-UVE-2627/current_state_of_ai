# Current state of AI playbook

## Goals

1.  Develop a good understanding of AI

2.  Develop insight into how AI could be applied within your own context

## Schedule

| Day | **Topic**                                            |
|-----|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Tue | Guest lectures about AI in general                   |
| Wed | Guest lectures about applying AI within our field    |
| Thu | Applying AI yourself in a case study                 |
| Fri | AI in education and Demo day                         |

You can find a more detailed schedule on Learn

## Group work and individual work

You will work with a second team member throughout the entire course. Both team members must contribute equally to the work and be present. Should there be an imbalance, this will be taken into account in the assessment. You may indicate (via Learn) who you would like to work with. Because there may be an odd number of students, we as teachers will form the groups. We will take individual preferences into account as much as possible, but cannot guarantee that you will be placed with your first choice.

## Resit

Attendance during this week is mandatory. Only in exceptional circumstances of absence will we work with individual resits. If you miss this entire week, you will have to resit the course next year.

## Hackathon

Important. On Thursday there will be a hackathon in which you will develop an application using a well-thought-out AI setup. The table below lists possible applications you could build. Of course, you are also free to decide for yourself what you want to make.

| \#  | Project                            |
|-----|------------------------------------|
| 01  | CSV Explorer & Visualiser          |
| 02  | Team Shift Planner                 |
| 03  | Personal Budget Tracker            |
| 04  | Tournament Bracket Manager         |
| 05  | Inventory Dashboard                |
| 06  | Event Check-in App                 |
| 07  | Domain News Intelligence Dashboard |
| 08  | GitHub Project Health Monitor      |

## DEMO

On Friday, a demo will take place in the JRCZ. This will be set up as a marketplace. Decide for yourself how best to present it. Companies have been invited to the demo. They will give you feedback on the product and the process. So make sure these are clearly visible.

## Assessment

**Deadline**: The assessment takes place on Wednesday in week 3 (see schedule on Learn). Tuesday of week 2, 23:59, is the deadline for submitting the portfolio.

Based on the submitted portfolio, you will give a presentation in pairs explaining what you have done. Focus especially on the given guidelines, the work done during the Hackathon, and the feedback from the experts. Also take the grading criteria into account.

## Submitting and delivering your work

At the beginning of the week, we will create a GitHub repository for each group. You submit this repository as a zip file on Learn. This repo contains all your work: your code, prompts, and the portfolio. A template of the portfolio is available as Markdown in the repo. If you prefer a Word document, you can find one on Learn.
